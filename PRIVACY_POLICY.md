# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 11, 2026

BAC Assistant ("the app") helps students access BAC study materials, calculate grades, and practice with quizzes. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

## Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, the app uses Firebase Authentication and may receive your Google account name, email address, profile image, and an app-specific user identifier. This information is used to provide sign-in and account features.

The app may also process:

- Your selected education field, year, language, and app preferences.
- Quiz progress and results stored on your device.
- Technical, device, and advertising information collected by Google Mobile Ads when advertisements are displayed.

The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Provide authentication and account functionality.
- Remember your settings and improve your experience.
- Provide BAC documents, curriculum information, quizzes, and grade calculations.
- Display and measure advertisements.
- Protect the app from abuse and maintain reliable service.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-party services

The app uses services provided by Google and Firebase, including Firebase Authentication, Google Sign-In, Firebase App Check, and Google Mobile Ads. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

BAC documents and curriculum information may be retrieved from remote servers so that the app can provide current content.

## Data storage and retention

Some preferences, quiz data, and cached content are stored locally on your device. Authentication information is handled by Firebase. We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the app and its features, or as required by law.

You can remove locally stored app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of account-related information, email [dzbacassistant@gmail.com](mailto:dzbacassistant@gmail.com).

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The latest version will be published with the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, email [dzbacassistant@gmail.com](mailto:dzbacassistant@gmail.com).
